This paper introduces a hybrid NLP framework combining monolingual BERT embeddings with 61 language-specific sociolinguistic features for detecting reclaimed slurs across English, Spanish, and Italian. The system ranked 1st/24 runs on the Italian subtask (Macro F1 = 0.8981).

**Contribution (Ghahremani, K.):** Cross-lingual sociolinguistic feature framing, design of identity-marker and pragmatic complexity features, and qualitative cross-lingual error analysis — identifying the *Identity Trap* (English agentivity confusion) and *Structural Mimicry* (Italian genre confusion) as principal failure modes.
