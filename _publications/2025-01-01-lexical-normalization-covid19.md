This first-author study examines how 29 translators rendered English coroneologisms (COVID-19-related neologisms) into Persian equivalents. The research applies Newmark's (1988) normalization taxonomy and Molina & Albir's (2020) translation strategy framework to a corpus of COVID-19 terminology.

**Key findings:** Blends and derived words were systematically normalized, while old words with new meanings proved the least transferable type — revealing how morphological structure conditions translation strategy choice in crisis-era neologism transfer.
