Presentation of the Ghavidel-Rajabi-Ghahremani team's shared task submission for the MultiPRIDE task on multilingual reclamatory intent detection. The system ranked 1st out of 24 competing runs on the Italian subtask (Macro F1 = 0.8981), combining monolingual BERT embeddings with 61 language-specific sociolinguistic features.
