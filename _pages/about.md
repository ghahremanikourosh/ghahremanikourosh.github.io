I am a linguist with a strong theoretical grounding in translation studies, pragmatics, and cross-cultural discourse analysis, now actively developing skills in computational approaches to language. I contributed to a peer-reviewed paper at EVALITA 2026 on multilingual reclamatory intent detection, where my background in sociolinguistics and translation theory informed the cross-lingual feature design and error analysis. I am currently building proficiency in Python and machine learning to pursue research at the intersection of linguistic theory and natural language processing.

- **Research Interests:** Computational Sociolinguistics, Multilingual NLP & Hate Speech Detection, Translation Theory, Cross-lingual Pragmatics, Literary and Critical Theory.
- **Recent Work:** Co-authored a multilingual hate-speech detection system (English, Spanish, Italian) that ranked 1st/24 runs on the Italian subtask (Macro F1 = 0.8981) at EVALITA 2026 MultiPRIDE.
- **Background:** M.A. in Translation Studies (Kharazmi University of Tehran), B.A. in Translation Studies (IAUH). Published first-author research on lexical normalization of COVID-19 neologisms in Persian translation.

> "Language shapes how we think, feel, and argue — understanding its structure is the first step to understanding its power."
